    Keep it lovely

    Mug care

    01

    How should I wash a glazed mug?

    Wash it with warm water, mild dish soap, and a soft sponge. A soft cloth works well for drying.

    02

    Can I use the microwave or dishwasher?

    Check the care instructions for your specific mug. If the maker hasn’t confirmed either use, hand-wash it and heat your drink separately.

    03

    How do I avoid sudden temperature changes?

    Let a cold mug come to room temperature before filling it with hot coffee or tea. Let a hot mug cool before rinsing it with cold water.
